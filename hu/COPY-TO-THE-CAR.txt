Copy onto the head unit USB (/fs/sda0):

  speeduntrap              ->  /fs/sda0/speeduntrap
  radar/points.txt         ->  /fs/sda0/radar/points.txt
  radar/chime.wav          ->  /fs/sda0/radar/chime.wav
  radar/map.bin            ->  /fs/sda0/radar/map.bin     (~1.1 GB)
  start_speeduntrap.sh     ->  /fs/sda0/start_speeduntrap.sh
  install_boot.sh          ->  /fs/sda0/install_boot.sh     (once)
  rollback_boot.sh         ->  /fs/sda0/rollback_boot.sh

Keep Unix LF on the .sh files. Then SSH:

  mount -uw /fs/sda0
  mkdir -p /fs/sda0/radar
  chmod 775 /fs/sda0/speeduntrap /fs/sda0/start_speeduntrap.sh /fs/sda0/install_boot.sh
  /fs/sda0/speeduntrap

Optional auto-start:  /fs/sda0/install_boot.sh   then   shutdown -S reboot

Full instructions: README.md in the parent folder.
